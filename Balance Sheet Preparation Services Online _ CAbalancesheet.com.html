Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common questions. Ask us if yours isn’t here.

What is a balance sheet?
A statement of a business’s assets, liabilities, and equity on a given date.
Can I get a balance sheet prepared online?
Yes. You share the required records remotely and we coordinate the process online.
Do you also prepare a profit and loss statement?
Where it is included in the agreed scope, yes.
What is a projected balance sheet?
An estimate of future financial position based on stated assumptions, often used for planning and financing.
Can every balance sheet be CA-certified?
No. Certification depends on the records provided, the checks required, and the professional’s eligibility and standards.
What is UDIN?
A Unique Document Identification Number generated by a practising Chartered Accountant for certain certifications, where applicable and permitted.
What is CMA data for a bank loan?
Credit Monitoring Arrangement data: financial statements, projections, and working capital details that banks commonly ask for.
What documents are required for balance sheet preparation?
Commonly a trial balance or ledgers, bank statements, details of assets and liabilities, and sales, purchase, expense and loan records. The exact list depends on your business and the purpose.
How much does balance sheet preparation cost?
Fees depend on the scope, complexity, completeness of your records and reporting period. Request a quote and we will confirm the fee before work begins.
Can you prepare CMA data for working capital finance?
Yes, we prepare CMA data to support eligible working capital and business loan applications. The lender decides on approval.
How long does the process take?
It depends on the service and how quickly complete records are shared. We confirm an estimated timeline when we agree the scope, but we can’t promise a fixed date.
How can I submit documents securely?
Please don’t send sensitive documents through the enquiry form. We’ll share an appropriate secure channel after you contact us.
Is bank loan approval guaranteed?
No. Approval depends on the lender’s own assessment. Document preparation does not guarantee it.
